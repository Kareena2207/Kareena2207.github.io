Deep Patch Visual Odometry Ablation Study

Benchmarked DPVO architecture variants on TartanAir to study temporal attention, ConvLSTM, and Softmax temperature tuning.

  • Replicated published DPVO results within a few centimeters.
  • Evaluated 16 sequences on an NVIDIA RTX 4090.
